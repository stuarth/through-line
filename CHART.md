# Chart

Create a route; do not begin resolving it.

1. **Charter the effort.** State the destination and its observable result. Record
   decision rights: what remains human-owned and what reversible choices the builder
   may make. Interview the human only while the destination or material domain
   boundaries remain unclear.
2. **Set execution posture.** Mark repository execution in or out of scope. For
   execution, record the first end-to-end result and the human's expected active
   effort to reach it, in the unit the human naturally uses. This is the point to
   rechart if the route has produced no path evidence, not a delivery deadline.
3. **Load settled commitments.** Reuse existing adopted principles and settled facts.
   Record only charter-level premises in the charter; leave their full authority in
   the decision or artifact that established them. Propose a new principle only when
   one commitment would collapse several independent choices, following
   [PRINCIPLES-GUIDE.md](./PRINCIPLES-GUIDE.md).
4. **Map in proof order.** Create only units warranted by the rule in
   [Through-line](./SKILL.md#model), connect their actual prerequisites, and end in an
   outcome that states the destination. For execution, make the first executable
   outcome a thin complete path across the components to an externally observable
   result. Record plausible in-charter work whose necessity is not established in
   **Deferred** with its owner and activation evidence; put excluded work in **Out of
   scope**.
5. **Confirm the charter and route.** Show the smallest credible route, what it will
   prove, and what waits. The human confirms the charter and initial direction.
   Later discovery may reshape units inside that charter; changes to the charter
   return to the human.
6. **Persist and stop.** Create the route and units through the configured tracker or
   [local Markdown](./trackers/local-markdown.md), validate them, and hand the first
   unit to a fresh [Advance](./ADVANCE.md) session.
