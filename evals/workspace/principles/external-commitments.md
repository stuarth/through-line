# External commitments
Status: adopted
Authority: Fictional workspace owner; [decision A](../decisions/adoptions.md#decision-a).

## Commitment

Describe a capability as available only when its present behavior has been verified.
Keep roadmap directions and illustrative examples separate from current customer
promises. A polished mockup is not evidence of an available capability.

## Why this choice

We accept a less impressive announcement rather than making the support team carry
an unintended promise. The rejected alternative treated planned capabilities as
available once their public explanation was ready.

## Applies when

Preparing recommendations, replies, announcements, proposals, demonstrations, and
other artifacts that state what customers can use or rely on.

## Boundary

A clearly labeled hypothetical design exploration may discuss unbuilt behavior.
Internal exploration does not itself promise delivery or authorize publication.

## Evidence

[Decision A](../decisions/adoptions.md#decision-a) records the fictional adoption.
Operating notes distinguish the fixture's shipped and planned behavior.
