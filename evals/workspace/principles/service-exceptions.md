# Bound service exceptions
Status: adopted
Authority: Fictional workspace owner; [decision C](../decisions/adoptions.md#decision-c).

## Commitment

A customer-specific concession records the beneficiary, extent, and end condition.
It does not change the standard offer unless that broader change is explicitly
adopted. Make the limited scope clear in resulting customer communication.

## Why this choice

We accept some administration to prevent an account-level accommodation becoming
an accidental promise to everyone. Extending the standard offer is a separate choice.

## Applies when

Offering credits, extensions, extra assistance, or other departures from the standard
offer, and revising guidance describing those offers.

## Boundary

A deliberately adopted change to the standard offer is not an exception. This record
does not set concession amounts or grant permission to send messages or change accounts.

## Evidence

[Decision C](../decisions/adoptions.md#decision-c) records the fictional adoption.
